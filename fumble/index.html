Questions, answered

How do I play?

Tap TRUE or FALSE on each of today's five claims, choose Power or Flex, lock your ticket, then watch the legs flip one by one. One ticket per local day.

What's the difference between Power and Flex?

Power: hit all five or walk away with nothing. Flex: miss at most one and still bank a smaller bag. Five of five pays more.

What is the Supporter Pack?

An optional one-time purchase that unlocks bag skins that theme the app, a Supporter crest, and extended stats. It never changes today's ticket, odds, or payouts, and it isn't required to play.

Do I need an account?

No. There's no account and no ads — your streak and bank stay on this device.
