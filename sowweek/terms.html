What the app does

SowWeek turns the seeds you add into a season plan. It dates each crop from frost dates: either NOAA NCEI U.S. Climate Normals 1991–2020 for the weather station nearest the ZIP code you enter, or the dates you type yourself. It uses typical crop timings, or the numbers you enter from your seed packet, and lists the jobs that fall in each week. Growth stages, sprouting times and pick counts in the app are illustrations based on those dates and on what you tick; they are not measurements of your plants.

Frost dates and planting advice

  • The dates are long-term averages for a weather station, which may be some distance from your garden and at a different height. Your garden’s microclimate can differ.
  • The app does not forecast the weather, detect frost, or know what is happening in your garden.
  • Seed packets, local extension services and your own experience may give better dates for your conditions. Where they differ, use your judgement.
