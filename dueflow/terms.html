The app

TestDue is an offline due book and test log for backflow prevention assemblies. You are responsible for how you use tickets, gauge readings and shared images. The app is a log only and is not a filed test report, licensed certification or AHJ approval. Always follow applicable plumbing and water-purveyor rules, manufacturer instructions and the law. Some cities require filing in a portal such as Syncta or BSI — TestDue does not replace that.
