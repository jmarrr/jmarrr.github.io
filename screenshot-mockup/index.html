Questions, answered

What does it do?

It places a screenshot inside a device frame on the background you choose, at its own resolution — never stretched — ready to post, pitch or present.

What kind of frames are they?

The frames are drawn by the app as simple shapes and fit iPhone and iPad screenshots. Any other size gets a simple rounded frame that keeps its exact shape.

Are my files uploaded?

Never. It all happens on your iPhone or iPad — no upload, no account, no server.

Is it a subscription?

No. Choosing a screenshot, styling it and seeing the finished image are free. One $4.99 purchase unlocks saving and sharing, for good. No ads.
