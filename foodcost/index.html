Questions

Is this a full kitchen suite like Fillet?

No. FoodMargin is a food cost calculator — recipe cost cards, food cost % and menu pricing only. No inventory counts, no ordering, no POS. Deliberately smaller and cheaper than a $30/mo suite.

How is the suggested price calculated?

Cost per serving divided by your target food cost %. Set a 30% target and a $2.05 plate suggests $6.84 — adjust the target per dish if you like.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Everything — ingredients, recipes, costing, export — runs on the phone. A walk-in cooler or a festival pitch with no signal changes nothing.

What do I get for free?

Five recipes with everything included: nested prep, yields, and the suggested sell price. Pro removes the recipe cap and the watermark on exported cost cards.
