Questions, answered

How many photos can I join?

Two to twelve, in one horizontal row, in the order you set from left to right. It is not a grid of hundreds of pictures.

What if two photos barely overlap?

Where two photos show the same scene, Panoform lines that overlap up. Where they do not, it sets them side by side with a hard edge. It does not flip or warp either frame.

What does the purchase unlock?

Arranging the row and the preview cost nothing. The preview's long edge is 1,200 pixels. The purchase unlocks saving and sharing the panorama at the full size of the photos you picked.

Do the photos leave the device?

No. Alignment and the finished picture stay on the iPhone or iPad. There is no account and no upload. Panoform does not detect faces or identify anyone.
