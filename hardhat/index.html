Questions, answered

What does the report actually look like?

US Letter PDF with your company name, licence number and logo at the top, then your before-and-after pairs side by side, each with its date and location stamp and your caption underneath.

Does it work without a connection?

Yes. Shooting, organising and building the PDF all happen on the phone. You only need a connection when you actually send the finished report.

Where are my photos stored?

On your device. Hardhat has no account and no server, so we never receive them. If you allow it, a copy of each photo is also saved to a Hardhat album in your own Photos library, so your normal device backup keeps a copy.

What do I get for free?

Three reports every calendar month, with a small footer credit. Pro removes the cap and the credit, adds your logo, and adds GPS and compass stamps.

Can I cancel?

Any time, from your Apple Account settings. Cancelling stops the renewal — the jobs and photos already on your phone stay yours.
