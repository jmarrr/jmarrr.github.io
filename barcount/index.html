Questions

Is this Partender or Backbar?

No. BarCount is a solo bar inventory count — shelf-order counting, variance, and a restock list, offline. No seats, no cloud dashboard, no ordering platform, and nothing like $165/mo.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The walk-in, the basement cage, a festival bar — counting, closing and the variance ticket all run on the phone.

Do I have to enter costs?

No. Counts and bottle variance work without them. Add pack costs when you want the ticket to show dollars walked, not just bottles.

What do I get for free?

One area with 25 bottles — and the whole point works on it: same-as-last counting, variance vs last close, the restock list, and your last 8 closed counts. Pro removes the caps and adds the clean owner PDF.
