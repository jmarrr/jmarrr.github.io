BarCount — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

BarCount keeps your work on your phone. There is no account and no advertising. Your counts, SKUs and area lists stay on your device — we never receive them and cannot see them. The app also sends anonymous usage counts — which screens are used and how often, sent each time you leave the app (from version 1.0.3; earlier versions sent them once a day) — to our own server, so we can see what gets in the way and fix it. No identifiers, no third parties, and you can switch it off in Settings → Privacy.

What the app stores, and where

Everything BarCount creates is written to on-device storage on your iPhone: areas, SKUs, closed counts, receives and optional JSON backups you export. None of it is transmitted to us.

Purchases

BarCount Pro is an auto-renewing subscription sold through Apple’s In-App Purchase system. Apple processes the payment and tells the app whether a subscription is active. We never see your payment details, and we do not receive your name, email or Apple Account information.

Share and export

If you share a variance report image or export a JSON backup, iOS hands the content to whichever app or service you choose. We are not involved in the transfer and receive no copy.

Device identifiers

BarCount does not use tracking SDKs or advertising identifiers. Local preferences use on-device storage only.

Anonymous usage counts

The app sends daily counters to a server we run ourselves, each time you leave the app (from version 1.0.3; earlier versions sent one batch a day): how often the app was opened, whether the core job was done, and how the subscription screen was used (shown, and where; plan selected; closed; purchases restored), each count tagged with its day and with how long ago the app was installed (the same day, 1–3, 4–7, 8–14, 15–30, or more than 30 days). Each post also carries the app’s name and version, your device language and your App Store country (the store your Apple Account uses, not your location); our server keeps those only as separate daily tallies (how many posts came from each version, language and country), not attached to the counts. There are no identifiers of any kind (no account, no advertising ID, no device ID), no job content, and no third-party analytics service. We use it only to see which parts of the app get in the way. Turn it off any time in Settings → Privacy; nothing is sent while it is off.

Children

BarCount is not directed at children under 13. It is a business inventory tool for adults who manage bar stock.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above.
