Questions, answered

What does it calculate?

The tonnage of hot mix to order (with a waste allowance built in), the number of truckloads rounded up to whole trucks, the loose volume in cubic yards, and the total job cost from your price per ton.

Does it work without signal?

Yes. It works completely offline, so it's fine on a job site with no signal — and it collects no data about you at all.

Is the density right for my mix?

Standard hot mix runs about 145 lb/ft³, but every mix is different. Set the exact density your supplier quotes and confirm it with them before you order.

What's free, and what does the unlock add?

You can try it free on a single area. One $4.99 purchase adds multi-section jobs, sharing and custom mix settings. No subscription.
