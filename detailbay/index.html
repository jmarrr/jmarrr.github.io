Questions

Is this a full CRM like Detail Connect?

No. DetailBay is a job log and before/after proof report tool — deliberately smaller and cheaper than an all-in-one suite with booking and SMS.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Shooting, organising and building the PDF all happen on the phone. You only need a connection when you send the finished report.

Where are my photos?

On your device. If you allow it, a copy also goes to a DetailBay album in your own Photos library.

What do I get for free?

Three proof reports every calendar month, with a small footer credit. Pro removes the cap and the credit, and adds your logo.
