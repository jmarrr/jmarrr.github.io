Questions, answered

Is it real money?

No. Symbolic pots climb from $100 toward $1M across 15 rungs. They are display only — no real money, no cash-out, no gambling.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no account and there are no ads. Your streak and biggest bag stay on this device.

What is the Supporter Pack?

An optional one-time purchase that unlocks a Supporter crest, extended stats and a nicer share card. It never changes today's chain, odds or pots.
