CuffBook — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 September 2026

Blood-pressure readings stay on this device. There is no account and no server for your log. Optional Apple Health access and optional anonymous usage counts are described below. We do not sell data and we do not use it for advertising.

What the app stores, and where

Readings you type (systolic, diastolic, pulse, time, arm, position, notes), profiles, reminder times, and CSV/JSON backups you export are written to on-device storage on your iPhone or iPad. We never receive them and cannot see them.

Apple Health

If you turn on Apple Health in Settings, CuffBook can read and write blood-pressure samples (and nearby heart-rate samples) so this log matches the Health app — for example, a 128/82 mmHg reading you already saved from your cuff. Health access is optional. The app works fully if you decline. Readings are not uploaded. You can revoke access in Settings → Privacy & Security → Health.

Anonymous usage counts

If “Share anonymous usage counts” is on (it defaults on; you can turn it off), CuffBook posts daily totals of product events — app open, first reading saved, paywall view, plan select, restore — with a coarse days-since-install bucket. There is no install ID, no advertising identifier, and no readings, names, or Health samples in that post. DEBUG builds do not post. Off stays off.

Purchases

CuffBook Pro is an auto-renewing subscription sold through Apple In-App Purchase. Apple processes the payment and tells the app whether a subscription is active. We never see your payment details.

Share and export

If you share a doctor-report PDF or export CSV/JSON, iOS hands the file to whichever app you choose. We are not involved in the transfer and receive no copy.

Children

CuffBook is not directed at children under 13. It is a logbook for adults tracking readings from their own cuff, or logging for a parent they care for.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above.
