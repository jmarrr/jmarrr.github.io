LastClean — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

LastClean keeps your work on your phone. There is no account and no advertising. Building names, addresses, alarm codes, zone lists and close notes stay on your device. We never receive them and cannot see them. From version 1.0.1 the app also sends anonymous usage counts (which screens are used and how often) to our own server, so we can see what gets in the way and fix it. No identifiers, no third parties, and you can switch it off in Settings.

What the app stores, and where

Everything LastClean creates is written to on-device storage on your iPhone: buildings, zones, last-night lines, close timestamps, optional JSON backups you export, and share images you generate. None of it is transmitted to us.

Location, camera and face data

LastClean does not request location, camera or contacts. It does not run face detection or any on-device Vision pipeline. Close times are the device clock, not GPS. Alarm codes are text you type; they stay under the hold-to-show flap and are stripped from shared cards.

Third-party and AI services

LastClean uses no analytics SDK and no third-party AI service. Apart from the anonymous usage counts below, it sends nothing over the network; purchases are handled by Apple's StoreKit on the device.

Purchases

LastClean Pro is an auto-renewing subscription sold through Apple’s In-App Purchase system. Apple processes the payment and tells the app whether a subscription is active. We never see your payment details, and we do not receive your name, email or Apple Account information.

Share and export

If you share a closet-card image or export a JSON backup, iOS hands the content to whichever app or service you choose. We are not involved in the transfer and receive no copy. Shared cards never include the alarm code.

Device identifiers

LastClean does not use tracking SDKs or advertising identifiers. Local preferences use on-device storage only.

Anonymous usage counts

From version 1.0.1, if “Share anonymous usage counts” is on in Settings, the app sends daily totals of a few product events to a server we run ourselves: app opened, first building added, subscription screen shown (and where), plan selected, subscription screen closed, and purchases restored. Each total carries a coarse days-since-install bucket; the post also includes the app’s name and version, your device language and your App Store country. There are no identifiers of any kind (no account, no advertising ID, no device ID) and no building names, addresses, alarm codes, zones or notes, and no third-party analytics service. We use it only to see which parts of the app get in the way. Turn it off any time in Settings; nothing is sent while it is off.

Children

LastClean is not directed at children under 13. It is a business tool for adults who close commercial buildings at night.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above.
